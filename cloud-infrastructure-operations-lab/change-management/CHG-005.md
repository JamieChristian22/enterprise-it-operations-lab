# CHG-005: Rotate operational KMS key policy

- **Risk:** High
- **Status:** Approved
- **Owner:** Jamie Christian II
- **Implementation window:** 2026-07-01 02:00–03:00 UTC

## Business justification
Improve reliability, security, recoverability, or operational visibility without changing customer-facing functionality.

## Implementation plan
Updated key policy and validated backup access. Validate syntax, capture pre-change state, apply through version-controlled tooling, and observe health metrics for 30 minutes.

## Test plan
Confirm resource health, application HTTP 200 response, expected monitoring signal, and no increase in error rate or latency.

## Rollback plan
Restore prior version-controlled key policy. Revalidate service health and document the reason for rollback.

## Approval
Approved by Cloud Operations lead after peer review of implementation, testing, and rollback steps.
