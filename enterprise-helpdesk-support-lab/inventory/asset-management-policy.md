# Asset Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy ensures that technology assets are acquired, recorded, secured, assigned, maintained, audited, returned, and disposed of in a controlled manner.

## Policy Requirements

1. Every managed asset receives a unique asset ID.
2. Serial number, model, status, location, owner, and lifecycle dates must be recorded.
3. Endpoints must be encrypted and security-health validated before assignment.
4. User assignment requires documented acceptance and condition records.
5. Returned devices must be inspected, sanitized, and reassigned or disposed.
6. Warranty expirations must be reviewed at least quarterly.
7. Repairs must include cost, vendor, warranty status, and resolution.
8. Software licenses must be tracked by entitlement and assignment.
9. Quarterly inventory audits must reconcile physical assets to records.
10. Devices leaving service must receive approved secure data erasure.
11. Disposal certificates must be retained.
12. Lost or stolen assets require immediate incident and security escalation.

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Asset Manager | Maintains inventory controls and reporting |
| Service Desk | Handles assignment, return, repair, and record updates |
| Security Operations | Defines encryption and wipe requirements |
| Procurement | Maintains purchase and warranty records |
| Department Manager | Confirms user ownership and return |
| Finance | Supports capitalization and disposal controls |
