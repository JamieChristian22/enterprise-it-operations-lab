# Post-Incident Review Template

## Summary

Document the incident, priority, duration, users affected, and service outcome.

## What Went Well

Record successful detection, coordination, escalation, communication, workaround, and recovery activities.

## What Did Not Go Well

Record delays, missing documentation, access problems, unclear ownership, monitoring gaps, or failed assumptions.

## Root Cause and Contributing Factors

Reference the completed RCA and summarize the operational learning.

## Corrective Actions

List each action with accountable owner, due date, status, and expected benefit.

## Final Decision

Record service owner acceptance, problem record linkage, change record linkage, and closure approval.
