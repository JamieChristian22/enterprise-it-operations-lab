# Incident Summary Template

## Incident Overview

Document the incident ID, title, priority, affected service, start time, detection time, restoration time, and closure time.

## Business Impact

Describe the affected users, departments, service disruption, productivity loss, and available workaround.

## Technical Summary

Explain the symptoms, confirmed cause, containment actions, recovery steps, and validation.

## Communications

List the initial notification, status updates, resolution notice, and service owner confirmation.

## Follow-Up

Reference related problem, change, monitoring, knowledge, or corrective-action records.
