# Vendor Escalation Template

## Case Summary

Record vendor case ID, product, version, entitlement, affected service, priority, and business impact.

## Technical Evidence

Include logs, timestamps, screenshots, reproduction steps, configuration details, diagnostic bundles, and failed actions.

## Troubleshooting Completed

Document internal testing and why vendor assistance is required.

## Requested Vendor Action

State the exact question, defect review, hotfix, configuration guidance, or engineering escalation required.

## Communication and Ownership

Record internal owner, vendor owner, next update deadline, workaround, and escalation path.
