# Shift Handoff Template

## Current Operational Status

Summarize service health, major incidents, critical alerts, and scheduled changes.

## Priority Work

List each open P1/P2 ticket, owner, current status, next action, and update deadline.

## Pending Items

Document customer responses, vendor cases, approvals, maintenance windows, and aged tickets.

## Risks

State any approaching SLA breach, service degradation, staffing gap, or unresolved security concern.

## Handoff Confirmation

Record outgoing analyst, incoming analyst, handoff time, and acknowledged ownership.
