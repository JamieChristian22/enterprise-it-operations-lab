# Monthly Service Review Template

## Executive Summary

Summarize service performance, business impact, major achievements, and primary risks.

## KPI Review

Report SLA, FCR, resolution time, CSAT, reopen rate, escalation rate, availability, and backlog.

## Operational Review

Summarize incidents, changes, problems, monitoring, security, assets, and knowledge management.

## Improvement Plan

List corrective and continual-improvement actions with owners, dates, status, and expected outcomes.

## Decisions and Acceptance

Record service owner decisions, risk acceptance, funding requirements, and agreed priorities.
