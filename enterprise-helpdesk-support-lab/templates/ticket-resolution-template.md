# Ticket Resolution Template

## Issue Summary

Provide one concise sentence describing the technical issue and business impact.

## Environment

Document the user, department, device, operating system, application, network context, and affected service.

## Troubleshooting Performed

Record each action in chronological order:

1. Confirmed the reported symptom.
2. Verified the scope and business impact.
3. Reviewed relevant logs, settings, and service health.
4. Tested the most likely causes.
5. Eliminated unrelated causes.
6. Applied the corrective action.
7. Performed technical and user validation.

## Root Cause

State the confirmed technical cause or the most likely cause when definitive proof is unavailable.

## Resolution

Describe the exact configuration, repair, reset, update, or workaround applied.

## Validation

Document the test performed, expected result, actual result, and user confirmation.

## Closure

State the final status, closure time, related records, knowledge article used, and any follow-up action.
