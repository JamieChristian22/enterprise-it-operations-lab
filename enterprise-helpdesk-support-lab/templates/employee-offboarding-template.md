# Employee Offboarding Template

## Employee Details

Record employee name, department, manager, termination date, and last working time.

## Identity Actions

- Disable account
- Revoke active sessions
- Reset credentials
- Remove privileged access
- Remove security and distribution groups
- Remove VPN access
- Block mailbox sign-in
- Configure mailbox retention or delegation

## Asset Recovery

- Recover laptop, mobile device, badge, and accessories
- Update assignment records
- Inspect condition
- Secure device
- Begin wipe or reassignment process

## Validation

Confirm access removal, manager approval, data retention, asset recovery, and final closure.
