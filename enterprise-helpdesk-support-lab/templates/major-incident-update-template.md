# Major Incident Update Template

**Subject:** Major incident update — Service impact under investigation

The IT team is actively investigating a high-impact service interruption.

Current status:

- The affected service and user scope have been confirmed.
- Technical teams are engaged.
- A workaround is being evaluated or communicated.
- Restoration remains the primary objective.
- The incident timeline and technical actions are being documented.

The next update will be issued according to the incident communication schedule.
