# Knowledge Base Article Template

## Title

Use a clear task or symptom-based title.

## Audience

State whether the article is for end users, Service Desk analysts, or administrators.

## Symptoms

Describe the observable issue, error, and affected environment.

## Cause

State the confirmed or likely cause.

## Resolution

Provide exact, ordered steps with expected results after each major action.

## Validation

Explain how to confirm the issue is resolved.

## Escalation Criteria

State when the user or analyst must stop and escalate.

## Related Records

Reference tickets, incidents, problems, changes, scripts, or policies.
