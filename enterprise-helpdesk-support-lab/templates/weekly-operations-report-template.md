# Weekly Operations Report Template

## Performance Summary

Report tickets opened, tickets resolved, SLA attainment, first-contact resolution, backlog, CSAT, major incidents, and critical alerts.

## Highlights

Document completed improvements, high-value resolutions, successful changes, and knowledge articles published.

## Risks and Issues

Document aged tickets, repeat incidents, unavailable services, staffing concerns, license risks, and asset constraints.

## Next-Week Priorities

Assign each planned action an owner and due date.

## Leadership Decisions

List approvals, budget, staffing, vendor, or risk decisions required.
