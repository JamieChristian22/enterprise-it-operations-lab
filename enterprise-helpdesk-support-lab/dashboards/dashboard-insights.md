# Dashboard Insights and Recommendations

## Key Findings

### Service Demand
Identity, Outlook, VPN, and Microsoft 365 support generate the greatest ticket volume. These areas offer the strongest opportunities for automation, self-service, and knowledge management.

### Service Quality
Improving SLA and customer satisfaction trends indicate stronger operational consistency. Reopened tickets should still be reviewed because a fast closure is not successful when the issue returns.

### Escalation
Escalation rate should be analyzed by category and priority. Higher escalation in critical or infrastructure incidents may be appropriate; high escalation for routine tickets may indicate a coaching or documentation gap.

### Compliance
Endpoint compliance and phishing accuracy improved across the three-month period. These metrics should remain part of the operating review because they connect Help Desk activity to broader security outcomes.

### Knowledge Management
Knowledge article usage is useful only when it improves resolution time, first-contact resolution, and consistency. Future reporting should compare tickets with and without knowledge usage.

## Recommendations

- Build self-service flows for password reset and account unlock.
- Publish targeted Outlook and VPN troubleshooting articles.
- Create a reopened-ticket review process.
- Add a weekly aged-backlog action list.
- Compare technician performance using complexity-adjusted measures rather than raw ticket volume.
- Add future KPIs for ticket deflection and knowledge article helpfulness.
