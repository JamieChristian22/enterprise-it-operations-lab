# Dashboard Assumptions and Limitations

- All data is simulated.
- SLA targets vary by priority: Critical 1 hour, High 4 hours, Medium 8 hours, and Low 24 hours.
- SLA Met is a prepared operational field representing response and resolution compliance.
- CSAT is available only for resolved or closed tickets.
- Endpoint compliance and phishing accuracy are treated as sampled support-security measures.
- Technician workload counts assignments and does not adjust for ticket complexity.
- Open backlog is measured as of August 1, 2026.
- Monthly trends cover May, June, and July 2026.
- The dashboard is designed to demonstrate reporting technique rather than claim production performance.
