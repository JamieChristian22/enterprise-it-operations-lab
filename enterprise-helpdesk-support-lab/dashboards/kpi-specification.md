# KPI Specification

## Objective

Provide service desk leadership with a clear view of demand, throughput, quality, customer experience, operational risk, compliance, and team workload.

## Required Views

1. Executive KPI cards
2. Tickets by category
3. Tickets by priority
4. Technician workload
5. Tickets by intake channel
6. Monthly ticket trend
7. Monthly SLA trend
8. Quality and compliance trend
9. Backlog aging
10. Management interpretation

## Required KPIs

- Tickets opened
- Tickets resolved or closed
- SLA attainment
- First-contact resolution
- Average resolution time
- CSAT
- Reopen rate
- Escalation rate
- Backlog
- Endpoint compliance
- Phishing accuracy
- Knowledge article usage

## Acceptance Criteria

- All KPI cards are formula-driven.
- Three months of source data are included.
- Dashboard values reconcile to source data.
- Unresolved tickets do not distort resolution metrics.
- A data dictionary and assumptions document are included.
- Charts are readable and do not overlap source data.
- Management recommendations are documented.
