# Monthly Service Desk Dashboard Summary

## Reporting Period

May through July 2026 simulated operations cycle.

## Executive Summary

The service desk processed **234 tickets** and resolved or closed **179**. Overall SLA attainment was **90.6%**, first-contact resolution was **90.5%**, average customer satisfaction was **4.43 out of 5**, and the reopen rate was **10.6%**.

## Trend Assessment

- SLA performance improved across the reporting period.
- Endpoint compliance and phishing accuracy increased each month.
- Knowledge article usage strengthened, supporting more consistent troubleshooting.
- Password, Outlook, VPN, and Microsoft 365 incidents remained the main demand drivers.
- Reopened and escalated tickets should be reviewed by category and technician for coaching or process improvement.

## Management Priorities

1. Expand self-service for password and Outlook issues.
2. Review all reopened tickets weekly.
3. Escalate backlog items older than 72 hours.
4. Continue technician workload balancing.
5. Maintain endpoint compliance at or above 95%.
6. Track knowledge article effectiveness, not just usage.
