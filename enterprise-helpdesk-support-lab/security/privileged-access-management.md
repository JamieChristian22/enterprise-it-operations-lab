# Privileged Access Management

## Controls

- Separate administrative and standard accounts
- MFA required
- No shared administrator credentials
- Privileged access assigned through approved groups
- Quarterly privileged access review
- Dormant accounts disabled
- Local administrator membership minimized
- Administrative activity logged
- Emergency access documented and reviewed

## Q3 Review Outcome

Four quarterly reviews examined local administrator membership, Microsoft 365 privileged roles, VPN access, and Finance shared folders. Seven unnecessary access assignments were removed.
