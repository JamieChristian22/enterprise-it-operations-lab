# Ransomware Response Playbook

1. Isolate affected endpoints and network segments.
2. Disable compromised accounts.
3. Declare a major security incident.
4. Preserve logs and forensic evidence.
5. Identify affected systems and data.
6. Block known indicators.
7. Protect backups from further access.
8. Rebuild or restore from verified clean backups.
9. Rotate affected credentials.
10. Validate recovery and monitoring.
11. Conduct legal, regulatory, and executive notification as required.
12. Complete post-incident review.
