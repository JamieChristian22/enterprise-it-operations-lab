# Acceptable Use Policy

Users must:

- Use company devices and accounts only for authorized business activity.
- Protect passwords and MFA methods.
- Lock devices when unattended.
- Report suspicious emails, unexpected MFA prompts, and lost devices.
- Store business data only in approved systems.
- Avoid installing unapproved software.
- Avoid connecting unapproved storage devices.
- Follow data-handling and confidentiality requirements.

Prohibited activity includes credential sharing, bypassing security controls, disabling endpoint protection, unauthorized scanning, and transmitting confidential data through personal accounts.
