# SLA Performance Report

## Results

| Month | SLA Attainment | Target | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| May | 88.9% | 90.0% | Below target |
| June | 92.3% | 90.0% | Met |
| July | 94.0% | 90.0% | Met |

## Drivers of Missed SLA

- Delayed escalation for complex VPN cases
- Authentication issues requiring cross-team coordination
- Aged tickets pending requester responses
- High-priority incidents that required infrastructure changes

## Corrective Actions

- Introduced daily aged-ticket review.
- Added 30-minute high-priority escalation checkpoint.
- Added certificate-expiry monitoring.
- Added M365 and VPN escalation runbooks.

## Conclusion

SLA performance improved for two consecutive months and exceeded target in July.
