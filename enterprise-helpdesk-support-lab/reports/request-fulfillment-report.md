## Assessment

Routine identity requests were completed efficiently. New-user and equipment requests met operating expectations but remain dependent on complete manager approvals and asset availability.

## Improvements

- Standardize access-request approval templates.
- Maintain one assignment-ready laptop in stock.
- Automate password and account-unlock workflows where approved.
