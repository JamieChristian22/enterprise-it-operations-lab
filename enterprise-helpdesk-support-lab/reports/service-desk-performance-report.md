# Service Desk Performance Report

## Reporting Period

May through July 2026.

## Performance Trend

| KPI | May | June | July | Direction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Tickets Opened | 72 | 78 | 84 | Demand increasing |
| Tickets Resolved | 54 | 60 | 65 | Throughput increasing |
| SLA Attainment | 88.9% | 92.3% | 94.0% | Improving |
| First Contact Resolution | 72.2% | 75.0% | 78.5% | Improving |
| Avg Resolution Time | 6.42h | 5.84h | 5.22h | Improving |
| CSAT | 4.31 | 4.39 | 4.52 | Improving |
| Reopen Rate | 9.3% | 8.3% | 6.2% | Improving |
| KB Usage | 51.4% | 57.7% | 63.1% | Improving |

## Analysis

Ticket demand grew by 16.7% from May to July. Resolution performance also improved, but resolved volume did not fully match intake. Continued backlog management is necessary.

The strongest positive trend is the combination of higher FCR, lower resolution time, lower reopen rate, and greater knowledge use.

## Recommended Actions

- Expand self-service for password and account-lockout requests.
- Add Outlook and VPN decision trees to technician workflows.
- Review backlog older than 72 hours daily.
- Measure ticket deflection from knowledge articles.
