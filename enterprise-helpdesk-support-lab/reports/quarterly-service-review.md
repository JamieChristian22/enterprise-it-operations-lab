# Quarterly Service Review — May to July 2026

## Business Outcome

The Service Desk supported increasing demand while improving SLA, first-contact resolution, resolution speed, customer satisfaction, and knowledge usage.

## Key Results

- Ticket demand increased 16.7%.
- July SLA reached 94.0%.
- July FCR reached 78.5%.
- July CSAT reached 4.52.
- Reopen rate fell to 6.2%.
- All major incidents were restored within target.
- Change and alert closure performance reached 100%.

## Strategic Priorities

1. Increase self-service.
2. Reduce VPN and M365 service risk.
3. Optimize software licenses.
4. Expand knowledge effectiveness reporting.
5. Maintain asset and monitoring audit discipline.
