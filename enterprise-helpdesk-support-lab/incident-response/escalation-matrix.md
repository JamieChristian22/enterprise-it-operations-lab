# Incident Escalation Matrix

## Functional Escalation

| Incident Type | Initial Owner | Escalation Team |
|---|---|---|
| Active Directory | Service Desk | Systems Administration |
| Microsoft 365 | Service Desk | Microsoft 365 Administration |
| VPN / Remote Access | Service Desk | Network / Infrastructure |
| Endpoint Failure | Desktop Support | Systems Administration |
| Security Event | Service Desk | Security Operations |
| Cloud Service | Service Desk | Cloud Operations |

## Management Escalation

| Priority | First Escalation | Second Escalation | Executive Notification |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 10 minutes | 20 minutes | 30 minutes |
| P2 | 30 minutes | 60 minutes | As required |
| P3 | 4 hours | 1 business day | No |
| P4 | 1 business day | 3 business days | No |

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate immediately when:

- Scope expands
- Recovery target is at risk
- A security control is affected
- A workaround fails
- Multiple teams are required
- Vendor intervention is needed
- Customer impact becomes material
