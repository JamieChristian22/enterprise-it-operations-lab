# Severity and Priority Matrix

## Impact Levels

| Impact | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Enterprise | Organization-wide service disruption |
| High | Multiple departments or critical service affected |
| Medium | One department or several users affected |
| Low | Single user or noncritical function affected |

## Urgency Levels

| Urgency | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Critical | Immediate restoration required |
| High | Major productivity or business risk |
| Medium | Workaround exists; moderate impact |
| Low | Minimal disruption |

## Priority Matrix

| Impact \ Urgency | Critical | High | Medium | Low |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Enterprise | P1 | P1 | P2 | P2 |
| High | P1 | P2 | P2 | P3 |
| Medium | P2 | P2 | P3 | P3 |
| Low | P3 | P3 | P4 | P4 |

## Priority Definitions

### P1 — Critical

- Organization-wide or critical-service outage
- No viable workaround
- Executive visibility
- Immediate response required

### P2 — High

- Significant business disruption
- Multiple users or one department affected
- Workaround may exist
- Rapid response required

### P3 — Medium

- Limited impact
- Workaround available
- Standard operational response

### P4 — Low

- Minor issue
- Minimal business impact
- Can be scheduled through routine support
