# Alert Severity Matrix

| Severity | Criteria | Response Objective | Incident Assessment |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Critical | Active service outage, severe malware, enterprise impact | 10 minutes | Required |
| High | Material degradation, security-control failure, multiple users | 30 minutes | Required when user impact exists |
| Medium | Limited degradation or compliance issue | 2 hours | As needed |
| Low | Informational or isolated condition | 1 business day | Not normally required |
