# Change Management Policy

## Purpose

Ensure infrastructure and service changes are evaluated, approved, scheduled, implemented, validated, and documented consistently.

## Requirements

- Every change must have an owner.
- Every normal and emergency change must identify affected services and configuration items.
- A rollback plan is required unless technically impossible.
- Testing and validation steps must be defined before approval.
- Stakeholders must be notified according to impact.
- Emergency changes require retrospective review.
- Failed changes require a post-implementation review.
- Evidence must be retained according to audit policy.
