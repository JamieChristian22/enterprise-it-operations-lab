# Change Types and Risk Model

## Standard Change

- Low risk
- Repeatable
- Documented procedure
- Pre-approved
- No material business disruption expected

## Normal Change

- Requires risk assessment
- Requires approval
- May require CAB review
- Scheduled on the forward calendar

## Emergency Change

- Required to restore service, address critical risk, or prevent severe impact
- Expedited approval
- Retrospective CAB review required

## Risk Score

| Factor | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business impact | Low | Moderate | High |
| Technical complexity | Simple | Moderate | Complex |
| User scope | Small | Department | Enterprise |
| Rollback difficulty | Easy | Moderate | Difficult |
| Testing confidence | High | Medium | Low |

Total risk score:

- 5–7: Low
- 8–11: Medium
- 12–15: High
