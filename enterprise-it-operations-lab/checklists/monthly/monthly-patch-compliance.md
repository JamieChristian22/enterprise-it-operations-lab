# Monthly Patch Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Confirm asset inventory.
- [ ] Calculate compliance by platform.
- [ ] Review critical and high-severity missing patches.
- [ ] Review devices pending reboot.
- [ ] Review approved exceptions.
- [ ] Validate exception expiration dates.
- [ ] Confirm failed deployments are resolved.
- [ ] Publish compliance report.
- [ ] Escalate compliance below target.
