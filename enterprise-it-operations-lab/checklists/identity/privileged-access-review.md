# Privileged Access Review Checklist

- [ ] Export privileged-role membership.
- [ ] Confirm each account owner.
- [ ] Confirm business justification.
- [ ] Confirm separate admin account use.
- [ ] Confirm MFA where supported.
- [ ] Remove dormant or unapproved access.
- [ ] Review emergency accounts.
- [ ] Record approver and review date.
- [ ] Retain evidence.
