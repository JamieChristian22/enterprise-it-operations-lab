# Major Incident Checklist

- [ ] Confirm major-incident criteria.
- [ ] Assign incident commander.
- [ ] Open communication bridge.
- [ ] Identify technical leads.
- [ ] Freeze conflicting changes.
- [ ] Record timeline.
- [ ] Communicate at defined intervals.
- [ ] Restore service.
- [ ] Validate business operation.
- [ ] Schedule root-cause review.
