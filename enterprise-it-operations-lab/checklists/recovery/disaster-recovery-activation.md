# Disaster Recovery Activation Checklist

- [ ] Confirm disaster criteria.
- [ ] Authorize DR activation.
- [ ] Notify business and technical leadership.
- [ ] Establish command structure.
- [ ] Recover identity and DNS.
- [ ] Recover network connectivity.
- [ ] Recover critical applications.
- [ ] Validate data integrity.
- [ ] Confirm business acceptance.
- [ ] Track RTO and RPO.
- [ ] Plan return to normal operations.
- [ ] Complete after-action review.
