# Retention Standard

| Backup Type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Hourly critical workload backup | 24 hours |
| Daily backup | 30 days |
| Weekly backup | 12 weeks |
| Monthly backup | 12 months |
| Annual archive | 7 years |
| Immutable copy | 14–30 days minimum |
| Security incident preservation | Per legal and security direction |

Retention must align with legal, operational, and business requirements.
