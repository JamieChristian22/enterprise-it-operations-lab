# KPI Definitions

- **SLA attainment:** Tickets resolved within the applicable service target.
- **First-contact resolution:** Tickets resolved without escalation or repeat contact.
- **Average resolution time:** Mean minutes from ticket opening to resolution.
- **CSAT:** Average customer satisfaction score from 1 to 5.
- **Patch compliance:** Compliant assets divided by all in-scope assets.
- **Backup success:** Successful jobs divided by scheduled jobs.
- **Change success:** Changes completed without failure.
- **MTTR:** Mean time to restore service.
