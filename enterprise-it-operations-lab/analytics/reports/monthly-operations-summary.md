# Monthly IT Operations Analytics Summary — July 2026

## Executive Summary

Northstar Services maintained strong operating performance in July. Service desk SLA attainment remained high, patch compliance exceeded the 95% target overall, and backup success remained strong. The most visible risks are the Rocky Linux patch exception and rising cloud spend.

## Key Findings

- Service desk SLA attainment: **88.9%** across the sample period.
- Average ticket resolution time: **87.9 minutes**.
- First-contact resolution: **61.1%**.
- Average CSAT: **4.56 / 5**.
- Overall patch compliance: **97.4%**.
- Backup success: **98.9%**.
- Change success rate: **80.0%**.
- Average major-incident MTTR: **25.0 minutes**.
- Monthly cloud cost increased from **$13,300** in April to **$14,575** in July.

## Recommendations

1. Review the Rocky Linux exception and confirm compensating controls.
2. Investigate the failed normal change and strengthen pre-production dependency testing.
3. Analyze AWS and Azure cost growth before year-end capacity decisions.
4. Increase first-contact resolution by expanding knowledge-base and automation coverage.
