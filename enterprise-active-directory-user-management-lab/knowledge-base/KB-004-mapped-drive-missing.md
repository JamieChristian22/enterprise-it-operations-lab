# Mapped Drive Missing After Sign-In

**Audience:** Internal IT Support  
**Owner:** Jamie Christian  
**Review cycle:** Quarterly

## Purpose

Run Group Policy update, inspect gpresult, verify item-level targeting, confirm the UNC path, and sign out/in after group changes.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the ticket contains a clear business request and approved requestor.
2. Verify the user identity and affected account or resource.
3. Collect facts before making a change.
4. Apply the least-privilege fix.
5. Validate from the user's perspective.
6. Document commands, outcome, and any follow-up.

## Escalate When

- Privileged access is involved.
- The request lacks approval.
- The issue affects multiple users or systems.
- Suspicious activity or possible compromise is present.
- The documented procedure does not restore service.

## Resolution Standard

The ticket is complete only when the user or a technical test confirms the expected outcome and the final notes contain no passwords or sensitive secrets.
