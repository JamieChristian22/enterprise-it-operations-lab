# Runbook: Suspicious Administrative Activity

1. Preserve audit logs.
2. Identify account, host, source IP, command, and time.
3. Contact the account owner through a trusted channel.
4. Disable or restrict the account when compromise is suspected.
5. Revoke sessions and rotate credentials.
6. Review recent privileged changes.
7. Escalate confirmed compromise.
8. Record evidence and remediation.
