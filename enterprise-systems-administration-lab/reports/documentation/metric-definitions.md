# Metric Definitions

## Availability

Percentage of scheduled service time during which the service is operational.

## Patch Compliance

Percentage of in-scope assets that have installed all approved updates within the required timeline.

## Backup Success Rate

Successful backup jobs divided by total scheduled backup jobs.

## MTTA

Mean time from incident creation to acknowledgment.

## MTTR

Mean time from incident creation to service restoration.

## Change Success Rate

Completed changes without rollback, incident, or unplanned impact divided by all completed changes.

## Capacity Headroom

Remaining usable capacity before reaching the operational warning threshold.

## Certificate Risk

Certificates expiring within the defined warning period, revoked certificates still deployed, or certificates using disallowed cryptography.
