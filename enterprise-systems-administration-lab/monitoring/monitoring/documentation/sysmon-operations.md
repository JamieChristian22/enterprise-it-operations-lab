# Monitoring Operations Guide

## Daily Operations

- Review active and recently resolved alerts.
- Review failed Prometheus targets.
- Review Grafana infrastructure overview dashboards.
- Confirm Alertmanager delivery.
- Review service availability and latency.
- Review disk and memory capacity trends.
- Confirm monitoring platform health.

## Weekly Operations

- Review noisy and low-value alerts.
- Review license, storage, and compute capacity.
- Review dashboard gaps.
- Test one runbook.
- Verify backup of Grafana and Prometheus configuration.
- Review unresolved recurring incidents.

## Monthly Operations

- Publish availability and capacity reports.
- Review SLO and error-budget consumption.
- Validate exporter and container versions.
- Test monitoring platform recovery.
- Review emergency contacts and escalation paths.
