# Knowledge Base Quality Checklist

A publishable article must pass every check:

- [ ] Title and permanent KB number are accurate
- [ ] Metadata is complete and current
- [ ] Symptoms are observable and specific
- [ ] Causes are technically plausible
- [ ] Commands are relevant to the platform
- [ ] Risk and prerequisite checks appear before changes
- [ ] Decision points prevent blind troubleshooting
- [ ] Validation proves both technical and business recovery
- [ ] Rollback is practical
- [ ] Escalation thresholds are explicit
- [ ] Security and data-redaction requirements are included
- [ ] Ticket example contains no secrets or placeholders
- [ ] Article is linked from README.md
- [ ] Register dates and status are current
