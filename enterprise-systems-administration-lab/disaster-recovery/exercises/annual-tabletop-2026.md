# 2026 Annual Disaster Recovery Tabletop

## Scenario
A ransomware incident compromises production identity, file services, and the primary backup management server while immutable backups remain intact.

## Discussion Outcomes
- Security containment precedes restoration.
- Recovery credentials are retrieved from offline escrow.
- Clean-room AD recovery is performed before file and application restoration.
- All privileged and service credentials are rotated.
- Business communications occur every 30 minutes.

## Gaps Found
- Application dependency sheet was not available offline.
- Alternate Incident Commander was not listed.

## Corrective Actions
Both gaps were remediated and validated before August 2026.
