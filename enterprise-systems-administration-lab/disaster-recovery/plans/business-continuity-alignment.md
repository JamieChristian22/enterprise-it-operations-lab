# Business Continuity Alignment

Disaster recovery restores technology; business continuity sustains business processes while technology is degraded. During recovery:
- Finance uses read-only exports for payment validation.
- Operations uses the emergency file share hosted at the recovery site.
- Help Desk uses the offline contact roster and priority communication template.
- Leadership receives updates every 30 minutes during Tier 0 outages.
- Business owners validate restored data before production use resumes.
