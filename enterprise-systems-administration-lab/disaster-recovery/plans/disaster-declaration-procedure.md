# Disaster Declaration Procedure

1. Open a P1 incident and bridge.
2. Record outage start time and affected services.
3. Confirm local recovery estimate.
4. Compare estimate with RTO thresholds.
5. Security Lead confirms whether containment is required.
6. Incident Commander declares DR activation.
7. Notify recovery team and business leadership.
8. Freeze nonessential changes.
9. Start recovery checklist and evidence capture.
10. Reassess every 30 minutes until exit criteria are met.
