# Enterprise Systems Administration Ticket Portfolio

**Author:** Jamie Christian  
**Environment:** Northstar Services Group — a fictional 650-user hybrid enterprise created for portfolio demonstration  
**Coverage period:** July 1–21, 2026  
**Ticket count:** 99 resolved incidents  
**SLA compliance:** 99 of 99 tickets met target  
**Purpose:** Demonstrate practical systems-administration troubleshooting, ITSM documentation, operational safety, and customer communication.

## Portfolio Scope

This folder contains complete incident records across Active Directory, Windows Server, Linux, Microsoft 365, virtualization, networking, backup and recovery, patching, security, storage, printing, and monitoring. The records use fictional names, systems, addresses, and results. They are designed to demonstrate job-ready procedures without representing access to a real employer environment.

## What Every Ticket Includes

- Complete ITSM metadata
- Priority, impact, urgency, and SLA
- Business impact and scope assessment
- Detailed investigation timeline
- Platform-specific commands
- Interpreted diagnostic findings
- Specific root cause
- Completed resolution
- Validation and customer confirmation
- Rollback and recovery readiness
- Preventive actions
- Linked knowledge article
- Sanitized evidence register
- Final closure note
