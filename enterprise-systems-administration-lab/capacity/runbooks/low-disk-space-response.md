# Low Disk Space Response

1. Confirm the affected volume and utilization.
2. Check active alerts and business impact.
3. Identify top directories using PowerShell.
4. Remove approved temporary files and expired logs.
5. Verify backup success before deleting archived data.
6. If utilization remains above 85%, open an emergency change for expansion.
7. Validate free space, service health, and monitoring recovery.
8. Document actions and root cause.
