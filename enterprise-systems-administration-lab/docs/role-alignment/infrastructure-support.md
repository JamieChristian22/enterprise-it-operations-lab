# Role Alignment — Infrastructure Support

## Most Relevant Capabilities

- Server health and service monitoring
- File, web, DNS, DHCP, and identity dependencies
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Capacity and performance analysis
- Incident, problem, and change records
- Operational reporting and escalation

## Best Evidence to Show

- Dependency mapping
- Monitoring outputs
- Capacity threshold responses
- DR exercise timelines
- Change validation and failed-change analysis

## Interview Positioning

Emphasize cross-system troubleshooting, dependency awareness, operational ownership, and the ability to communicate technical risk to stakeholders.
