# Role Alignment — Junior Systems Administrator

## Most Relevant Capabilities

- Active Directory, DNS, GPO, and delegated administration
- Windows Server services
- Backup and restore operations
- Patch and change management
- Monitoring and capacity planning
- PowerShell automation
- Configuration management with Ansible and DSC

## Best Evidence to Show

- AD health reports
- GPO validation
- Backup restore evidence
- Capacity forecasts
- Approved changes with rollback plans
- Configuration drift and idempotency reports

## Interview Positioning

Describe the environment as a controlled enterprise simulation. Focus on how changes were validated, how failures were handled, and how documentation supports reliable operations.
