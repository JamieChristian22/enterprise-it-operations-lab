# Document Lifecycle

## States

1. Draft
2. Technical Review
3. Approved
4. Published
5. Superseded
6. Retired

## Review Frequency

- Policies: annually
- Runbooks: every six months or after material change
- Recovery procedures: after every exercise
- Evidence indexes: monthly during active development
- Role-alignment guides: quarterly during job search

## Required Metadata

Each controlled document should identify its owner, purpose, review date, and related operational area. Major revisions must be summarized in the revision history.
