# Interview Talking Points

## Active Directory

“I designed administration around least privilege, delegated common support tasks, and validated DNS and replication after changes.”

## Backups

“I treated restore testing as the real measure of backup success. The project includes integrity checks, restore validation, retention, and ransomware recovery.”

## Change Management

“I documented implementation, validation, rollback, approvals, and post-implementation review. I also retained a failed change to show how I handled rollback.”

## Configuration Management

“I used Ansible and DSC to demonstrate repeatable server configuration, idempotency, drift detection, and remediation.”

## Capacity

“I used baselines and forecasts rather than waiting for outages. The reports connect utilization trends to specific remediation decisions.”

## Disaster Recovery

“I mapped dependencies and recovery order, then tested scenarios against RTO and RPO objectives.”
