# Sample Questions and Answers

## How did you validate Active Directory health?

I used DNS checks, replication summaries, DCDIAG-style validation, service status, and post-change authentication tests. I documented the results and linked them to the related change or runbook.

## What is the difference between a backup and disaster recovery?

A backup is a protected copy of data or system state. Disaster recovery is the coordinated process for restoring services, dependencies, access, and business operations within approved recovery objectives.

## Why use both Ansible and DSC?

Ansible provides centralized orchestration across systems, while DSC enforces a desired Windows configuration locally. Together they demonstrate deployment control, idempotency, and drift remediation.

## How do you reduce change risk?

I use scope definition, dependency review, risk scoring, approvals, pre-change health checks, implementation steps, validation criteria, rollback triggers, and post-implementation review.

## What would you improve next?

I would add automated documentation publishing, CI checks for scripts and playbooks, longer-term trend history, and additional vendor-specific integrations.
