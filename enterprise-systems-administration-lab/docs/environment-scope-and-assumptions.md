# Environment Scope and Assumptions

## In Scope

- One Active Directory forest and domain
- Two domain controllers with integrated DNS
- Windows file, web, monitoring, backup, and management servers
- Linux administration node
- Microsoft 365 and Entra ID integration concepts
- Virtualized workloads
- Centralized backup and recovery
- PowerShell, Ansible, and DSC automation
- Change, capacity, configuration, and disaster-recovery processes

## Out of Scope

- Production customer data
- Live payment systems
- Public-facing internet services
- Real corporate credentials
- Regulated personal information
- Vendor licensing procurement

## Assumptions

- The environment is isolated and used only for demonstration.
- Sanitized evidence represents realistic execution results.
- System names and addresses follow the documented naming standard.
- Administrative access is restricted to named roles.
- Business approvals are simulated but recorded with consistent timestamps and responsibilities.

## Limitations

The lab demonstrates process and technical capability but does not claim production employment experience. Vendor-specific interfaces may be represented through documented outputs, configuration examples, and sanitized evidence when a licensed product is unavailable.
