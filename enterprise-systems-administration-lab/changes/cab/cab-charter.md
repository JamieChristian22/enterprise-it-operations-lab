# Infrastructure Change Advisory Board Charter

## Mission
Review normal and high-risk infrastructure changes to confirm technical readiness, business alignment, resource availability, communications, rollback, and validation.

## Membership
- CAB Chair: IT Operations Manager
- Active Directory/DNS Lead
- Systems Administrator
- Network Administrator
- Security Analyst
- Service Desk Lead
- Business representative as required

## Cadence
Every Wednesday at 2:00 PM ET. Emergency CAB sessions may be called by the Incident Commander.

## Quorum
CAB Chair plus two technical members. High-risk changes require the affected service owner.
