# Change Implementation Runbook

1. Confirm approvals and maintenance window.
2. Notify stakeholders 30 minutes before start.
3. Capture pre-change health and configuration state.
4. Confirm backups, snapshots, and rollback prerequisites.
5. Execute each numbered implementation step.
6. Record timestamps and deviations.
7. Run post-change validation.
8. Monitor for 30 minutes or the service-specific period.
9. Communicate success or invoke rollback.
10. Attach evidence and close the change.
