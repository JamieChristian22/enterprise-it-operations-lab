# Post-Implementation Review Runbook

## Required for
Failed, backed-out, emergency, high-risk, or materially deviated changes.

## Review Questions
- Did the change meet its objective?
- Was the impact accurately predicted?
- Were implementation and validation steps sufficient?
- Was rollback successful?
- What caused failure or delay?
- What preventive action is required?

## Outputs
Lessons learned, updated runbook, problem record when needed, owner, and due date.
