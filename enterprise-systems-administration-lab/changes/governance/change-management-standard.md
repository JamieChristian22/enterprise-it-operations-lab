# Change Management Standard

## Lifecycle
Requested → Assessed → Approved → Scheduled → Implemented → Validated → Closed

## Required Fields
- Change ID and title
- Requestor and implementer
- Business justification
- Affected services and configuration items
- Risk and impact ratings
- Test results
- Implementation plan
- Validation plan
- Rollback plan
- Maintenance window
- Communications
- Approvals
- Outcome and evidence references

## Risk Scoring
Risk score = Impact × Likelihood, using a 1–5 scale.
- 1–4: Low
- 5–9: Moderate
- 10–15: High
- 16–25: Critical

## Approval Gates
- Low standard change: preapproved catalog owner
- Moderate normal change: service owner + CAB
- High normal change: service owner + security + CAB chair
- Critical change: CIO delegate + CAB chair + business owner
- Emergency: Incident Commander + IT Operations Manager
