# Change Risk Assessment Model

## Impact
1. No user impact
2. Limited team impact
3. Department impact
4. Multiple departments or critical service degradation
5. Enterprise-wide outage or regulatory exposure

## Likelihood
1. Proven repeatable procedure with automated validation
2. Tested procedure with low complexity
3. Moderate complexity or multiple dependencies
4. Limited testing, complex dependencies, or narrow rollback window
5. Unproven procedure, irreversible action, or critical unknowns

## Additional Risk Multipliers
Escalate one level when the change affects authentication, internet connectivity, backup integrity, security controls, or more than 100 users.
