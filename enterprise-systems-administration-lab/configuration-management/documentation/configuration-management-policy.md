# Configuration Management Policy

All managed Windows servers must have an approved baseline, named owner, configuration source, validation method, and recovery procedure. Production-equivalent changes require peer review and a change record. Credentials may not be committed to source control. Configuration drift classified as Critical must be remediated within four hours; High within one business day; Medium within five business days; Low during the next maintenance cycle.

Exceptions require a documented business reason, compensating control, owner, approval, and expiration date. Configuration evidence is retained for one year. Quarterly reviews measure coverage, drift rate, failed deployments, rollback rate, and mean time to remediate.
