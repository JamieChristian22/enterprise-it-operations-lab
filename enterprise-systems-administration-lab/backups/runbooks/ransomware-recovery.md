# Ransomware Recovery Runbook

1. Isolate affected systems and disable compromised credentials.
2. Preserve forensic evidence and notify the Security Lead.
3. Do not restore into an untrusted production environment.
4. Identify infection time and select a recovery point preceding compromise.
5. Build clean recovery infrastructure from trusted media.
6. Rotate privileged, service, and backup credentials.
7. Scan recovery points and restored systems before reconnection.
8. Restore identity services, then critical business services.
9. Monitor for indicators of persistence or reinfection.
10. Maintain offline and offsite copies until incident closure.
