# Runbook: Teams Access Issue

1. Confirm Team name, user, role, and exact error.
2. Confirm account and license status.
3. Confirm Microsoft 365 Group or Team membership.
4. Confirm the Team is not archived.
5. Check guest-user status when applicable.
6. Review service health.
7. Validate SharePoint site access.
8. Remove and re-add membership only after recording the original state.
9. Validate in the Teams web client.
10. Document the resolution.
