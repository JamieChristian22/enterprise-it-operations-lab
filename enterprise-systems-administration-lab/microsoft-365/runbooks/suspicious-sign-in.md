# Runbook: Suspicious Sign-In

1. Review the sign-in event, IP address, location, device, application, and authentication result.
2. Contact the user through a trusted channel.
3. Block sign-in when compromise is likely.
4. Revoke active sessions.
5. Reset credentials.
6. Review and remove unfamiliar authentication methods.
7. Review mailbox rules, forwarding, recent consent grants, and role assignments.
8. Review audit activity.
9. Re-enable the account only after containment and validation.
10. Record evidence, impact, and remediation.
