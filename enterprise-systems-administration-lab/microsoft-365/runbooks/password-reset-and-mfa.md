# Runbook: Password Reset and MFA Recovery

## Trigger
A verified user cannot sign in, forgot a password, or lost access to the registered MFA method.

## Procedure
1. Verify the user's identity using the approved process.
2. Review sign-in logs for risk or policy blocks.
3. Confirm the account is enabled.
4. Reset the password only after verification.
5. Revoke sessions when compromise is suspected.
6. Require password change at next sign-in.
7. Remove or reset authentication methods only when authorized.
8. Confirm MFA re-registration.
9. Validate access.
10. Document verification, actions, and result.

## Escalation
Escalate suspected account compromise, impossible travel, unfamiliar MFA registration, or privileged-account issues.
