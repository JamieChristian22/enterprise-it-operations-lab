# INC-004 — New Hire Could Not Be Licensed

**Severity:** High

## Cause
All Microsoft 365 Business Premium licenses were consumed, including five assigned to disabled accounts.

## Resolution
Licenses were removed from disabled accounts after mailbox and OneDrive preservation was confirmed. The new employee was then licensed.

## Validation
The license report showed available capacity and the mailbox provisioned.

## Prevention
Scheduled weekly license-capacity and disabled-user license reports.
