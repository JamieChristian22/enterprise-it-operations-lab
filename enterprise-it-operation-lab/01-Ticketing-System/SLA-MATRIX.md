# SLA Matrix

| Priority | Definition | First Response | Update Frequency | Target Resolution | Escalation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| P1 Critical | Company-wide outage, security event, or business-stopping failure | 15 min | 30 min | 4 hours | Immediate Tier 2 and leadership |
| P2 High | Multiple users or one executive unable to perform critical work | 30 min | 2 hours | 8 business hours | Tier 2 after 2 hours |
| P3 Normal | Single-user incident with workaround or standard service request | 4 business hours | Daily | 3 business days | Lead review after 2 days |
| P4 Low | Information request, cosmetic issue, or planned enhancement | 1 business day | Every 2 days | 5 business days | Review during queue audit |
