# Priority Matrix

| Impact / Urgency | High Urgency | Medium Urgency | Low Urgency |
|---|---|---|---|
| High Impact | P1 | P2 | P2 |
| Medium Impact | P2 | P3 | P3 |
| Low Impact | P3 | P3 | P4 |

**Impact** measures how many people or business services are affected. **Urgency** measures how quickly the business consequence will occur.
