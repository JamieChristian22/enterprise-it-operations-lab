# Priority and SLA Guide

| Priority | Definition | Initial Response | Target Resolution |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Critical | Organization-wide outage or severe security impact | 15 minutes | 2 hours |
| High | Core service unavailable for one or more business-critical users | 30 minutes | 4 business hours |
| Normal | Degraded service with workaround | 4 business hours | 2 business days |
| Low | Request, question, or minor inconvenience | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## INC-10487 Priority Rationale
The incident is High because a Finance user lost access to email and calendar, had no immediate workaround, and required restoration during business operations.

## Priority Reassessment
Lower priority only after a stable workaround is confirmed. Increase priority when user count, business impact, or security exposure expands.
